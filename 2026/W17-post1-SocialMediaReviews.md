## Summary

I’m launching **SocialMediaReviews**, a personal project to **analyze and document technologies from a European perspective**—focusing on technical depth, privacy, and real-world usability. To amplify its reach and foster discussion, I’ll start sharing these insights on social media (primarily LinkedIn and Twitter/X). This post explains the *why*, the *how*, and the *rules* I’ll follow to keep it valuable, neutral, and sustainable.

---

## Why This Project?

### The Gap
Most tech reviews today are either:
- **Marketing-driven**: Sponsored content or vendor-biased comparisons.
- **US-centric**: Ignoring European regulations (GDPR), hosting preferences, or local market needs.
- **Superficial**: Lacking benchmarks, code examples, or critical analysis of trade-offs.

As a European developer, I often struggle to find **objective, technically rigorous** evaluations of tools that fit our context. This project aims to fill that gap.

### Personal Motivation
- **Learn in public**: Documenting my research helps me retain knowledge and refine my understanding.
- **Give back**: Share insights that might save others time (or money) when choosing technologies.
- **Build a network**: Connect with like-minded engineers, especially in Europe, to exchange perspectives.

---

## What to Expect

### Content Focus
Each review will cover:
1. **Technical Analysis**: Performance, scalability, architecture, and limitations.
2. **European Lens**: GDPR compliance, data sovereignty, EU hosting options, and pricing in euros.
3. **Practical Comparisons**: Alternatives, hidden costs, and real-world use cases.
4. **Neutral Verdicts**: No sugarcoating—if a tool underperforms, I’ll say it.

### Topics on the Radar
| Category          | Examples                          |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Cloud/Hosting** | Hetzner, OVH, Scaleway vs. AWS/GCP |
| **Databases**     | PostgreSQL, SQLite, SurrealDB     |
| **Dev Tools**     | GitHub Actions, Woodpecker CI     |
| **Privacy**       | Self-hosted analytics, encryption |
| **AI/ML**         | Open-source LLMs, EU-based APIs   |

### Social Media Strategy
- **Platforms**: LinkedIn (long-form), Twitter/X (threads and hot takes).
- **Format**:
  - **Threads**: Deep dives (e.g., "Why I switched from Vercel to Cloudflare Pages for my EU projects").
  - **Polls**: Quick comparisons (e.g., "Which EU cloud provider do you use for GDPR compliance?").
  - **Code Snippets**: Practical examples (e.g., "How to configure PostgreSQL for GDPR data deletion").
- **Frequency**: 1–2 posts/week (mix of original content and curated insights).

---

## Rules of Engagement

To keep this project credible and useful:

1. **No Sponsorships**: I won’t accept payment or freebies for reviews. If I ever do (e.g., for early access to a tool), I’ll **disclose it explicitly**.
2. **Transparency**: All benchmarks will include methodology (e.g., "Tested on a 4-core Hetzner CX21 instance").
3. **Corrections Welcome**: If I get something wrong, call me out—I’ll update the post and credit you.
4. **No FOMO/Hype**: I’ll avoid jumping on trends just because they’re popular. Focus on **long-term value**.
5. **Respect**: No personal attacks, even if I criticize a tool. The goal is to **improve the ecosystem**, not burn bridges.
